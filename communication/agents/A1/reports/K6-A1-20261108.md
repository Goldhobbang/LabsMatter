- WASM: `glow_buffer_ptr/len`, `set_glow_enabled`, `glow_enabled`.

- WebGPU fragment shader: same idea with a second texture binding (implemented,
  unverified here — no adapter).
- Wall `eglow` contributes when powered; particles contribute fire colour.
